Adjust the running timer

Use these buttons to quickly change the remaining time while the timer is running.

Instructions

Enter a duration and click “Start”. Formats: “500” (seconds), “1m” (minute), “1h” (hour), “1d” (day).

When time is up, a notification and sound will alert you. You can minimize the app to the system tray while the timer keeps running.
