Adjust the running timer

Adjusting the timer: Use the buttons above (-1h, -10m, -5m, -1m, +1m, +5m, +10m, +1h) to quickly change the remaining time. They are enabled while the timer is running.

Instructions:

Enter a duration and click "Start".

Formats: "500" (seconds), "1m" (minute), "1h" (hour), "1d" (day).

When time is up, a notification and sound will alert you.

You can minimize the app to the system tray while the timer keeps running.
